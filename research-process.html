How I Work

Research Process

A repeatable framework, not a one-off ritual: from aligning on the question to building institutional knowledge that outlasts any single study.

01

Research Foundation + Alignment

Get stakeholders to agree on the question, and on the decision it will inform, before anything is designed.

Produces
Research brief with agreed scope and approach
See it in practice
Advisor Platform: competing stakeholder views on what to build →
02

Methodology + Research Design

Pick the method the question needs, then define exactly who has to take part.

Produces
Method plan, participant profiles and screener
See it in practice
Advisor Platform: MaxDiff, because ratings alone could not separate features →
03

Research Execution + Data Collection

Pilot first, then run sessions that build trust fast enough to get honest, unfiltered answers.

Produces
Pilot-tested discussion guide, session notes with behavioral observations
See it in practice
Auto Refill: 8 moderated sessions that surfaced a control concern →
04

Analysis + Synthesis

Code the patterns, use the right statistics, and reduce everything to a few insights ranked by impact.

Produces
Coded themes, utility scores, ranked findings
See it in practice
Advisor Platform: Hierarchical Bayes ranking of the candidate features →
05

Communication + Activation

Tell the story in the users' own words, and end with recommendations a team can act on.

Produces
Readout, recommendations, research playbooks
See it in practice
Schwab Learn: three recommendations on entry point, signposting, and completion →
06

Impact Measurement + Knowledge Management

Check whether the fix worked, and bank what was learned for the next study.

Produces
Measurement plan, follow-up research, reusable repository
See it in practice
Provider Survey: a retrospective built into every cycle →
