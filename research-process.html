How I Work

Research Process

A repeatable framework, not a one-off ritual: from aligning on the question to building institutional knowledge that outlasts any single study.

01

Research Foundation + Alignment

Align on the business question and get stakeholders on the same page before any research begins.

Strategic Discovery
  • Identify key business objectives and metrics through stakeholder interviews
  • Analyze competitive landscape for opportunities and benchmarks
Stakeholder Alignment
  • Conduct workshops to establish shared understanding of research goals
  • Document agreed-upon scope and approach in a research brief
02

Methodology + Research Design

Choose and combine the right methods, then define exactly who needs to take part.

Method Selection
  • Balance qualitative and quantitative approaches based on research objectives
  • Apply Jobs-to-be-Done frameworks for deeper understanding of user motivations
Participant Strategy
  • Define clear participant profiles based on user personas and segments
  • Leverage multiple recruitment channels appropriate to effort and demographics
03

Research Execution + Data Collection

Run the sessions, building trust fast enough to get honest, unfiltered feedback.

Preparation + Testing
  • Create research protocols and discussion guides with stakeholder input
  • Conduct pilot sessions to refine prompts and timing
Moderation Approach
  • Build rapport quickly to create psychological safety for honest feedback
  • Document behavioral observations alongside verbal feedback
04

Analysis + Synthesis

Turn raw data into a small set of insights the business can actually act on.

Data Processing
  • Organize qualitative data using thematic coding frameworks
  • Apply appropriate statistical analyses to quantitative data
Insight Development
  • Transform patterns into actionable insights through collaborative analysis
  • Prioritize findings based on impact to user experience and business metrics
05

Communication + Activation

Tell the story in a way that drives decisions, not a report that sits in a drive.

Storytelling
  • Craft compelling narratives that center the user experience
  • Use direct quotes to build empathy alongside the data
Recommendation Development
  • Develop research playbooks that standardize approaches across projects
  • Contribute to research operations improvements based on project learnings
06

Impact Measurement + Knowledge Management

Track whether the fix worked, and bank what was learned for the next study.

Outcomes Tracking
  • Establish measurement frameworks for tracking implementation impact
  • Conduct follow-up research to validate improvement effectiveness
Research Repository
  • Maintain a centralized, reusable research repository (personas, journey maps, frameworks)
  • Build institutional knowledge that informs future product decisions
